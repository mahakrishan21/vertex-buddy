# Reference studied: "Best AI Product Video for SaaS | Lovio" (Zelios)

https://youtu.be/pZv7me6dFns, 53 seconds. Studied on 2026-11-21 by stepping through it one frame per second in a headless browser and reading its auto-generated captions. Nothing from it is kept in this project, and its sound was not heard: the notes on voice come from the captions, and there are no notes on its music or effects.

## The narration

About 95 words in 53 seconds, in full flowing sentences, one thought per breath:

1. A question as the hook: "What if building software felt as effortless as having an idea?"
2. The name and what it is: "Meet Lovio, the AI platform that turns your ideas into living, working products."
3. How: "Just describe what you want and Lovio builds it instantly."
4. Range: "From landing pages to full-scale SaaS tools, everything you imagine comes to life."
5. Qualities, as a run of short words: "Beautifully designed and ready to launch, edit, refine, scale in any direction."
6. The feeling: "What starts as an idea becomes something people can use, share, and love."
7. A reframe: "Think less about how to build and more about what's next."
8. A closing question and the call: "So, what will you create today? Start building with Lovio."

The type on screen never repeats the whole sentence. It picks out two to four words ("idea", "Beautifully designed", "ready to launch", "an idea", "More about what's next", "create today") and lets the voice carry the rest.

## The picture

- **About 25 shots in 53 seconds**, most of them two seconds or shorter.
- **A new world almost every shot:** a dark logo stage, a photographed concrete wall with light shafts, purple gradient waves, a laptop on a desk, a hand holding a phone, a tablet on blue, a person typing, a glass prompt box, floating cards, a wall of generated sites, a white product page, a forest plate, a flower in 3D.
- **Scale keeps changing:** wide device shots alternate with close-ups of one control, and with type-only shots that act as breathers.
- **Something is always moving:** the camera drifts in every shot, interfaces build while we watch, text is typed, a cursor clicks.
- **A motif opens and closes it:** the flower mark at the start becomes the 3D flower and the logo at the end.

## What was taken from it, and what was not

Taken: the shape of the narration (question, name, how, range, feeling, close), type that picks out key words, a new ground for almost every shot, short shots, constant camera drift, close-ups alternating with wide shots, a calm dark passage before the ending.

Not taken, and why: photographs, people, devices and 3D objects (there is no footage or image source for them here, and Vertex Buddy has to be shown as it really is); its words, layouts and colours.
