# Reference studied: "Ecommerce Product Demo Video for Shopify | SKUVE" (Zelios)

https://youtu.be/POZ8qiJDEiI, 59 seconds. Studied on 2026-11-22 by stepping through it one frame per second in a headless browser. Nothing from it is kept in this project. Its sound was not heard and its captions were not read, so these notes cover the picture only.

## What it does

- **Three colours only:** white, near-black and one lime green. Grounds alternate between white and black from shot to shot.
- **Light, plain type that types itself:** a thin sans at moderate size, appearing letter by letter with a cursor bar. It is never bold or shouting.
- **Real footage, full frame, with type over it:** hands on a laptop, a woman at a desk, a hiker on a mountain.
- **Pictures set inside sentences:** "You have ( bag )( shoe )( tent ) the products", with small product pictures in the brackets.
- **Floating product cards** drifting at different speeds behind a short label, and later rows of cards scrolling in opposite directions.
- **Interface close-ups** with a hand cursor: a search field being typed into, quiz options being ticked, a slider dragged.
- **Footage in one colour** under a dot screen, used as a short transition.
- **Split screen:** three people in three panels, each with a green label ("Personal", "Fast", "Built into").
- **The mark builds from dots** on black, then two short typed lines and the address.

## What version 4 takes from it

The three-colour look (with Vertex's own neon green as the accent), typed text with a cursor, real footage with type over it, pictures inside a sentence, drifting cards, a one-colour footage transition, three labelled panels, rows of cards running opposite ways, an interface close-up with a cursor, and a mark that builds from dots.

Not taken: its words, layouts, footage and product.
